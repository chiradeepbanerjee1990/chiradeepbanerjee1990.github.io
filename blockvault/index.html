Built for people who actually like puzzles

Most block games run out of ideas by level fifty. This one has four goal types, obstacles that change how you plan, and a difficulty curve that was tuned by simulation rather than guesswork.

Four ways to win

Hit a score, clear a set number of lines, shatter frozen blocks, or dig out every energy core. Each one demands a different way of reading the board.

Combos that matter

Clear two lines in one move, or clear on back-to-back moves, and the multiplier climbs to x4. Chaining is where the big scores live.

Boss levels

Every tenth level is a Vault Run. Every twenty-fifth is an Overload — tighter move budget, higher target, and it will make you think.

Never a dead tray

The game will not hand you three pieces that cannot be placed. When you lose, it is because of a decision you made, not a bad draw.

Boosters, not paywalls

Hint reveals the strongest placement. Smasher destroys one block. Shuffle rerolls your tray. Earned with coins from playing.

Phone and tablet

One layout that adapts to the screen it is on. The board is always a centred square, never a stretched mess on a 12-inch tablet.

How it plays

Thirty seconds to learn. The app also ships with an illustrated tutorial and on-board coaching for the first three levels.

Drag a piece

Three pieces sit in the tray. Press one, drag it onto the grid, let go. Pieces never rotate, so pick the one that fits.

Fill a line

Complete a whole row or column and it clears, freeing up space for the next piece.

Beat the goal

Every level has a target and a move budget. Run out of either and the run is over.
