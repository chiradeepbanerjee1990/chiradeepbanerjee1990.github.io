Privacy Policy for Bubble Shooter: Bubble Pop

Applies to the Android app Bubble Shooter: Bubble Pop (package com.littlejoygames.bubbleshooter).
Last updated: September 20, 2026

Bubble Shooter: Bubble Pop is an offline-capable, single-player arcade puzzle game. There is no user account registration, no login, and no social networking integration. This policy explains what information the game handles locally on your device and how third-party services (such as Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform) handle data for advertising and privacy consent.

Who we are

Bubble Shooter is developed and published by LittleJoy Games, contactable at gameslittlejoy@gmail.com. In data protection terms, we act as the data controller for the limited information described below.

Intended audience & Children's Privacy

Bubble Shooter is intended for a general audience. It is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to third-party ad providers through the app, please contact us at the email address above so we can address your inquiry promptly.

Information the game stores locally on your device

All gameplay progression and customization data is stored exclusively on your device using Android's standard local storage (SharedPreferences / Jetpack DataStore). This data is never transmitted to our own servers and remains on your device:

  • Highest unlocked level and individual level star ratings (1 to 3 stars across 2,500 levels)
  • In-game virtual coin balance
  • Booster quantities owned (Bombs, Rainbow Wildcards, Color Nukes, Super Aim extensions)
  • Unlocked bubble cosmetic themes and active equipped theme (Classic Gloss, Candy Jelly, Cyber Neon, Metallic Chrome, Cosmic Nebula, Crystal Prism, Golden Royalty, Celestial Apex)
  • Sound effect, background music, and vibration/haptic preference settings

Uninstalling the app removes its local on-device data. If you have Android Cloud Backup enabled in your Google account settings, Google may retain a private backup of app preferences and restore them if you reinstall the app on a new or reset device.

Information collected for advertising

Bubble Shooter is free to play and supported by advertisements. We use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. To select, serve, measure, and protect against fraudulent ad activity, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process certain device and network data:

Data TypePurpose
Advertising ID (Google Play Services AD_ID) Ad serving, frequency capping, fraud prevention, measurement, and (where consented) personalization.
Approximate location (derived from IP address) Serving geographically relevant ads and enforcing regional privacy regulations (e.g. GDPR, CCPA).
Device and app specifications (model, OS version, language, display size) Ensuring visual ad compatibility and technical stability.
Ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks, completed rewarded video views) Rewarding in-game bonuses and detecting invalid traffic.

We do not collect or have access to your name, postal address, email, telephone number, contacts, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location.

Google's handling of ad data is governed by: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions used by the app

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to download and display ad creatives and retrieve consent forms.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Enables the app to check whether an active connection is available before requesting ads.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Provides tactile haptic feedback when bubbles bounce and pop (can be disabled in-game).
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Permits Google Mobile Ads SDK to use the standard advertising identifier for ad delivery and fraud prevention.

Consent and your privacy choices

GDPR / EEA / UK / Switzerland Consent: If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you are presented with a Google-certified consent form (via Google User Messaging Platform) when you first launch the app. You can accept, reject, or customize your advertising choices. You may review or update your consent preferences at any time within the game's Settings screen via the Privacy Options button.

Advertising ID Controls (All Regions): You can reset your Google Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising entirely on your Android device at any time by navigating to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). If opted out, you will still see ads, but they will be contextual rather than personalized.

Deleting Local Data: You can wipe all game progression at any time by going to your device's Settings → Apps → Bubble Shooter → Storage → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.

Legal bases for processing (GDPR / UK GDPR)

  • Consent: For personalized advertising and processing of user data for personalized ad delivery, requested via Google UMP.
  • Legitimate Interests: For non-personalized advertising, preventing fraud and invalid clicks, measuring ad delivery, and ensuring application security.

Data retention & Security

We do not store user data on external servers or maintain any account database. Advertising data collected by Google is retained according to Google's standard data retention policies. All communications with Google ad services take place over secure, encrypted HTTPS protocols.

Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the game following updates constitutes acceptance of the modified policy.
